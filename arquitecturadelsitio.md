You are a senior product designer and web architect building a modern personal brand website using Antigravity.

Design the full structure and functionality of a **personal website for an artist, creator, and mentor**.

The website must function as:

• Personal brand hub
• Video showcase
• Merch store
• Booking platform
• Portfolio
• Press / media resource

The experience should feel immersive and centered around the artist's personality.

---

PAGE STRUCTURE

Create a **single-page scrolling website** with the following sections.

---

HERO SECTION

Full-screen immersive hero.

Features:

• Full screen looping background video featuring the artist
• Cinematic visual impact
• Artist name and tagline overlay
• Minimal navigation
• Smooth scroll indicator

Goal: Immediately introduce the artist’s presence.

---

VIDEO CONTENT SECTION

Create a section dedicated to **video fragments and clips**.

Requirements:

• Grid or cinematic layout
• Short video clips displayed as cards
• Videos embedded from YouTube or hosted video
• Hover interaction that previews video

Purpose: showcase artistic video work.

---

BIOGRAPHY SECTION

Include a biography section.

Content:

• Portrait image
• Artist biography
• Artistic philosophy or statement

Tone: personal and expressive.

---

NEWSLETTER / COMMUNITY

Create a **stay connected** section.

Users should be able to:

• Subscribe with email
• Receive updates about launches
• Receive newsletters and announcements

Elements:

• Email input
• Subscribe button

---

SERVICES / CONSULTING

The artist offers **advisory sessions or mentorship**.

Include:

• Service cards
• Description of advisory sessions
• Book session button

Optional: scheduling integration.

---

SHOP SECTION

Include a merchandise store.

Products may include:

• Bracelets
• Rings
• T-shirts
• Hats

Requirements:

• Product cards
• Price display
• Add to cart functionality

The store should feel integrated with the artist brand.

---

PORTFOLIO / IMAGE CAROUSEL

Create a portfolio gallery section.

Include:

• Image carousel
• Artistic photography
• Portfolio visuals

Scrolling interaction or slider.

---

PRESS KIT / DOWNLOADS

Create a section where visitors can download media assets.

Examples:

• Press kit
• Media assets
• Press releases
• Wallpapers or screensavers

Display downloads as cards with file labels.

---

FOOTER

Include:

• Social media links
• Contact email
• Newsletter reminder
• Copyright

---

INTERACTION PRINCIPLES

The website must include:

• Smooth scrolling
• Subtle animations
• Interactive hover states
• Mobile-first responsiveness

Navigation should feel simple and fluid.

---

FINAL GOAL

Create a website that combines:

• Artistic expression
• Personal storytelling
• Commerce
• Community building

Visitors should feel like they are **entering the artist's creative world**.
